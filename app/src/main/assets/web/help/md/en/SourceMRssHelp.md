# RSS source management

RSS sources use rules to load article lists and content from feeds or sites.

- Import/create a source, name/group it and enable it as needed.
- Where Discovery exists, green/red indicators show its enabled/disabled state, as for book sources.
- Check article URLs, titles, dates and content before saving.
- Sign in on the phone when required by the site.
- RSS and book sources have different contracts; renaming a configuration does not convert its type.
