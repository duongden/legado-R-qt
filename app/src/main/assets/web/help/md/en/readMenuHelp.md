# Reading menus and settings

Tap the reading menu area to open tools. Use contents to navigate, book search to find text, and details to manage the book.

- Fonts: choose a local TTF/OTF font, size and spacing.
- Layout: adjust lines, paragraphs, margins/width, alignment and paging/scrolling.
- TTS: choose a configured engine/voice; speed support depends on the engine.
- Images/manga: use scrolling or zoom when images are scaled to a page.
- Source/JS actions depend on context and are not available for every book.
The web reader provides Contents/Settings and previous/next chapter controls. Interface language/colors are in shared settings. Wait for synchronization before switching devices.
